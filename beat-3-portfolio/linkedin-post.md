I recently had my first open source contribution accepted: I fixed a bug in spotatui, a music player that runs inside a terminal window. When someone typed a long playlist name or search, the text got cut off and the cursor wandered out of the box, so you couldn't see what you were typing.

The fix is now merged, and the maintainer added me to the contributors section of the project's README. It took about a day to plan and build and about a week to get through review.

Working in a language I had never used (Rust) taught me a few things:
✅ AI helped me map how the app fits together, so I found the right files quickly.
✅ I wrote tests that failed before the fix and passed after, which proved it worked.
✅ A step-by-step plan kept the work focused.
✅ A checklist I wrote caught a gap in my plan before I submitted.

Thank you to the maintainer for a quick, clear review.
